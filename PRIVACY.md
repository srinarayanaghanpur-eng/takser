# Privacy Policy — Sri Narayana Teacher Tasks

**Last updated: August 2026**

## Data We Collect

Employee ID, name, department, login credentials (email derived from employee ID), assigned tasks, task status updates, comments, proof photos/documents you attach, and push notification tokens.

## How We Use It

To assign and track teaching tasks, send deadline reminders and new-task alerts, measure completion performance, and manage staff accounts. Data is visible to school administrators.

## Data Storage

Data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore, Authentication, Storage) with role-based access rules. Only authenticated staff can access the app.

## Photos & Files

Proof photos and documents you attach are stored in Firebase Storage and visible to administrators for verification only.

## Notifications

We send task alerts via push notifications. You can disable them in your device settings; in-app alerts will still appear.

## Account Removal

Contact your school administrator to disable your account. Disabled accounts immediately lose access; task history is retained for school records.

## Contact

For privacy questions, contact the school administration office.
